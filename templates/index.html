A MESSAGE FROM THE FOUNDER

Every Student Deserves an Opportunity to Grow.

Education is not just about learning a subject. It is about building confidence, developing skills and creating opportunities for the future.

At Shree Ram Institute of Computer Science, our vision is to make computer education approachable and meaningful for students from Singla and surrounding areas of Balasore. We want every learner to explore technology, understand its possibilities and move forward with confidence.

We believe that talent exists everywhere. With the right guidance, practical knowledge and determination, every student can take a step towards a brighter digital future.
